The **Consumer Choice Center (CCC)** is an independent, non-partisan consumer advocacy organization championing freedom, innovation, and abundance in everyday life.

We advocate for public policies that expand consumer choice, encourage competition, enable innovation, and support economic growth around the world.

## What you’ll find here

This GitHub organization hosts selected CCC:

- Interactive research tools and dashboards
- Policy indices and structured datasets
- Public research methodologies
- Data-processing and visualization code
- Open resources supporting evidence-based policy analysis

## Featured project

### Autonomous Vehicle Access Tracker

The **Autonomous Vehicle Access Tracker** examines regulatory barriers affecting commercial driverless passenger services across the 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia.

The project includes source-linked policy classifications, market data, a transparent regulatory restrictiveness index, and an interactive research dashboard.

[View the AV Access Tracker →](https://github.com/Consumer-Choice-Center/ccc-ava-access-tracker)

## Our principles

Our work is guided by:

- **Consumer freedom** — People should have meaningful choices.
- **Innovation first** — New technologies and business models should have room to develop.
- **Evidence-based policy** — Regulation should be grounded in credible evidence.
- **Technology neutrality** — Public policy should focus on outcomes, not favor particular technologies.
- **Transparency** — Research methods, sources, and limitations should be clearly communicated.
